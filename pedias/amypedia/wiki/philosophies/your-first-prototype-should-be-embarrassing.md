# Your First Prototype Should Be Embarrassing

Amy's prototype philosophy: if the first version looks polished, you probably waited too long. Cardboard, hot glue, ugly CAD, quick tests, and awkward first passes teach faster than pristine renderings.

The deeper point is anti-preciousness. Make the thing bad enough that reality can answer back.
