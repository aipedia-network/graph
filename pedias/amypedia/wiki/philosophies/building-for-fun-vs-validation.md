# Building for Fun vs. Building for Validation

A major Amypedia pattern: some projects are built because they are alive and funny and tactile, while others get crushed under the need to prove something.

Cute Ghostty, Hardware Craft Night, and Capybara Gummies belong to the fun lineage. The lesson is not to avoid ambition. The lesson is to notice when ambition has eaten the play.
