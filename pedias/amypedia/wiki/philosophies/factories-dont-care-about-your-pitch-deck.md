# Factories Don't Care About Your Pitch Deck

A recurring Amy thesis: factories do not care how good the story is. They care about yield, cycle time, tolerances, materials, assembly, inspection, logistics, and whether the part can actually be made.

This philosophy anchors her public manufacturing posts and her professional interest in tools that connect design intent to manufacturing reality.
