# Kerf

Kerf is Amy's open-source B-rep CAD kernel in Rust, built for the AI era of design tools. It supports primitives like boxes, prisms, cylinders, cones, spheres, tori, and frustums, plus boolean operations, STEP export, and a CLI.

The project lives at https://github.com/amywork777/kerf and was submitted to YC Summer 2026 as a solo-founder application.

Why it matters: Kerf bridges mechanical-engineering instincts and software infrastructure. Instead of treating CAD as a closed black box, it asks what geometry tools should look like when AI agents need reliable, programmable representations of physical parts.
