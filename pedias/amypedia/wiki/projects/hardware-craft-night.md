# Hardware Craft Night

Hardware Craft Night was a hands-on event Amy hosted around small LED PCB charms. People made heart and flower-shaped boards into little glowing objects, without needing soldering experience.

The project is very Amy: manufacturing-aware, tactile, friendly, slightly cute, and grounded in the belief that tech people still want to use their hands.
