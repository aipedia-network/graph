# Personal Infrastructure

Amy's personal infrastructure is a set of small sites and systems for memory, content, stats, and coordination. The public-safe version includes Amypedia, content planning, life dashboards, and AIPEDIA participation.

Design language: warm paper, soft glass, soft-autumn colors, tiny rituals, and enough structure to make a chaotic life searchable.
