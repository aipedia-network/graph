# Stanford SHAPE Lab

The Stanford SHAPE Lab studies shape-changing interfaces, haptics, and physical interaction design. Amy's time around this world connects directly to her later obsession with the boundary between digital design and physical manufacturing.

It is one of the earlier public threads tying together mechanical engineering, interfaces, and physical-product taste.
