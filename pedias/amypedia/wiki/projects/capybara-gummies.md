# Capybara Gummies

Capybara Gummies is Amy's tiny consumer-product thought experiment: caffeinated gummies shaped like capybaras.

It is not important because it is a serious startup plan. It is important because it shows the fun version of Amy's product brain: manufacturing curiosity plus a cute object plus a reason to ask, "wait, could this be real?"
