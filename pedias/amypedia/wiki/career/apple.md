# Apple

Amy worked at Apple Softgoods as a Product Design Engineer during her Stanford years. Her Apple chapter connects her mechanical-engineering background to high-volume consumer hardware, textiles, accessories, and the kind of material-detail work that later shaped her public manufacturing voice.

She is a named inventor on Apple patent US 2025/0088581 A1, "Accessory Devices with Textile-Based Walls." Public patent link: https://patents.google.com/patent/US20250088581A1/en
