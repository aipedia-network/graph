# Craft, Music, and Style

Amy's non-work taste is part of the work, annoyingly enough. Pottery, handmade jewelry, clarinet, soft-autumn colors, cozy Y2K layers, and Pokemon HeartGold all sit in the same ecosystem as CAD and manufacturing.

## Clarinet

Amy has played clarinet since childhood, including youth orchestra and Stanford music contexts.

## Craft

She makes jewelry, does pottery, and keeps returning to hands-on objects because materials are where her thinking gets less abstract.

## Style

Soft autumn palette, hoodies, long skirts, cargo pants, tactile textures, and small-object curation.

## Pokemon

Her HeartGold team includes Typhlosion, Ampharos, Crobat, Graveler, Victreebel, and Slowpoke.
