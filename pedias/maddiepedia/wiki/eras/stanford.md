# Stanford Era

Maddie Wang attended Stanford University, with documented campus-based activity running at minimum from early 2022 through mid-2024. Evidence in the messages includes references to Stanford residential buildings (Munger, Wilbur, EVGR), campus locations (Gates building, d.school, Coho), and participation in Stanford-affiliated student organizations and events. Maddie's mentions of being a freshman and attending alcohol-scarce social events place her initial enrollment earlier, and by September 2022 she lived in Munger building 4, room 217, which she ranked as her first housing choice. By mid-2024 she described herself in the past tense as someone who "went to Stanford" and had transitioned to San Francisco.

## Coursework and Academic Identity

Maddie described herself jokingly as a CS major in the context of an urban studies pitch, suggesting a computer science background or identity on campus. She was involved in activities at the d.school (Stanford's Hasso Plattner Institute of Design) and the Gates computer science building, both of which she used as study and work spaces. In October 2022 she organized an outing tied to "Porn and the WICS boba," referencing the Women in Computer Science (WiCS) organization, and attended an event called "Sex then Socrates" in November 2022. She referenced attending Stanford's Startup Garage program in the context of a 2024 conversation. She had also joined or was connected to Founder's Café (founderscafe.notion.site), a Stanford-adjacent startup community.

## Housing

During at least the 2022-2023 academic year, Maddie lived in Munger Hall (building 4, room 217). She described choosing Munger first in housing selection. Lainey, her partner at the time, was also on campus, living or staying in EVGR. In spring and summer 2022, Maddie co-searched for a San Francisco apartment with [[people/ngoc]] and [[people/lainey]]; the three eventually moved into a flat at 1540 Fell Street in San Francisco, which they rented from an older landlord they described informally as "the old man." They split costs three ways, Maddie and Lainey sharing one portion and Ngoc taking another.

## Social Life

The Stanford social scene Maddie documented was highly event-driven. She attended talks, startup pitches, and events at the d.school, sometimes discovering free food and drinks there and alerting friends. She organized group outings for boba through WICS, invited friends to Electroluxx, a queer Pride party, and attended sex-education events including trivia nights. She also ran or contributed to a boba event at Zareens restaurant in October 2022, where she distributed "4 bobas and 6 mango lassis."

She was embedded in a queer social circle on campus. The [[communities/stanford-queer-girls]] group chat, named "3 Stanford Queer Girls," connected Maddie with [[people/ngoc]] and [[people/lainey]]. She referred to friends and peers by first name frequently (Kaiden, Chenlin, Carol, Mary) and organized group trips including a weekend at an Airbnb in nature for shrooms and an Airbnb in the Pinnacles area.

She described her freshman year as a period of social experimentation, including sneaking alcohol from parties into larger containers. She later reflected on this in conversation with [[people/amy-zhou]].

## Startup Activity

Maddie's startup orientation was visible throughout the Stanford period. She referenced Xfund (a Harvard-Stanford VC fund) as a dinner event she and Amy applied to. She ran Founder's Café (Buggo: founderscafe.notion.site), and by late 2022 and 2023 was working on a project later called StoryMage. She attended Startup Garage, a Stanford course in which teams develop ventures. She also joined or organized smaller pitching and networking events on campus.

## Transition Out of Stanford

By summer 2024, Maddie and [[people/amy-zhou]] were co-searching for a San Francisco apartment post-Stanford, eventually identifying a loft at 673 Brannan Street. Their correspondence from this period includes detailed planning for life after campus. Maddie noted she had grown tired of driving down to Stanford for laundry and that the campus felt behind her, though Amy still had PhD friends there with dining access. After leaving Stanford, Maddie described her trajectory as moving into tech, sales, and AI work in San Francisco.
