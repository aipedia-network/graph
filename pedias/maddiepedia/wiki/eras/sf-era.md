# SF Era

The SF Era refers to the period beginning in late 2023 when Maddie Wang established herself in San Francisco full-time and began running StoryMage as a primary venture. By September 2023, she announced to a friend that she was "back in SF for life" and had moved into the Mission neighborhood. The era is characterized by entrepreneurial activity, active social integration into San Francisco's queer community, a deepening then dissolving professional collaboration with Siraj Nazir ([[projects/ivylevel]]), and the end of the StoryMage chapter. Starting mid-2024, her primary social network expanded to include LA-based contacts from competitive dodgeball.

## Arrival and neighborhood

Maddie moved to the Mission District of San Francisco by September 2023, at the latest. This was distinct from the earlier 1540 Fell Street apartment documented in the Stanford era. In the same month she arrived, she sought connections to the local queer founder community, asking about Renee's number and attending or expressing interest in a "Prism launch party" in the city on October 11.

## StoryMage

[[projects/storymage]] was the primary professional focus during the first phase of this era. It launched its website at storymage.ai, operated a SocialPilot account for scheduling client LinkedIn posts, and charged a subscription model. By September 2023 it had its first customer at $800 per month. By October 2023 it had multiple clients including individuals from PearVC, PLA (People Leader Accelerator), and a marketing agency founder. By early February 2024, Maddie reported $1.2k MRR from a new organic marketing product and cited StoryMage's peak at $4k MRR in two months as a prior benchmark.

Lainey was a key collaborator throughout this period, contributing to content production and client management from within the same household.

## IvyLevel advisory

Alongside StoryMage, Maddie devoted significant time to advising [[projects/ivylevel]], a college admissions startup run by Siraj Nazir. This work included LinkedIn content strategy, product feedback, and meeting regularly with Nazir to review his go-to-market approach. Nazir referred to Maddie as a cofounder in at least one LinkedIn post. The collaboration ended acrimoniously in February 2024, with Nazir sending a lengthy message citing documentation of their communications and contesting Maddie's level of commitment. Maddie requested space and a pause on communication.

## Social life and queer community

Throughout 2023 and 2024, Maddie remained embedded in San Francisco's queer social community. She attended CrossFit classes and scheduled activities around them. She participated in queer founder events and circles, connecting with people affiliated with organizations like Lesbians Who Tech and startOUT. She described being in the Mission as allowing her to reconnect with queer social networks she valued.

The Birthday Bonanza planning chat (January 5, 2024) documents her friend group at this time: Amy, Lydia, Mohini, Carol, Mary, Ngoc, and Hillary, coordinated by Lainey. The group planned a wine-country Airbnb trip to celebrate Maddie's birthday.

## Disruptions and transitions

The car towing incident on [[events/car-towed-2024-01-05]] (January 5, 2024) captures a characteristic moment of this period: Maddie returning late from singing, low phone battery, discovering her car gone, messaging Lainey for support, and managing multiple ongoing professional commitments on the same day.

By mid-2024, Maddie's documented activity began to include LA. She organized a she/they dodgeball team for the OutLoud LA Sports Festival in August-September 2024, recruiting players from her San Francisco queer dodgeball community and connecting with new players including [[people/hua]] from Honolulu. This marked a significant expansion of her geographic social network beyond the Bay Area.

## Founders Cafe

Founders Cafe (FC) appears throughout this era as a structured community for early-stage founders. Maddie ran workshops on topics including conversion pitches and sales scripting. A co-organizer named Selena Secor is referenced in the December 2023 entries. Maddie shared a comparison of her lead generation results across channels, noting that forums and Slack communities significantly outperformed LinkedIn for conversions in her experience.

## Professional trajectory

By February 2024, Maddie was tracking her own progression across ventures: zero revenue from her first project (Sesame), $180 MRR in two months from Founders Cafe, $4k MRR in two months from StoryMage, and $1.2k MRR in two days from a new organic marketing product. These benchmarks were shared in a group chat as evidence of increasing speed to validation. By early 2026, entries reference $150,000 ARR, suggesting continued growth in her consulting or software work.
