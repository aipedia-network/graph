# High School and Early College

The earliest documented messages from Maddie Wang's iMessage history begin in January 2018. The conversations from 2018 and 2019 reveal a social world centered on the Twin Cities area of Minnesota (home) and Stanford University (school). The threads from this period open lines of contact with [[people/jon]], [[people/phoebe]], and [[people/sabrina]] that each persisted for years.

## Location and social context

The Minnesota area code (+1-651 and +1-425) contacts suggest Maddie's family home was in the Twin Cities. Messages from this period reference Rosedale (a shopping center in the Minneapolis suburbs), thrift stores, and local friends. Jon's message about Maddie having the family car keys in January 2018 and coordinating with "Dad needs the car" suggests shared use of a family vehicle while living at home or attending a nearby school.

By late 2018 and into 2019, messages reference Stanford locations (Tresidder Union, Axe and Palm, parking permits) alongside returns to Minnesota during breaks. The pattern suggests Maddie began at Stanford in fall 2018, with the Minnesota threads representing home-period contact.

## Friendships from this era

[[people/phoebe]] was a close friend and apparent roommate at Stanford. Their first documented message is from January 2018, before the start of the academic year, suggesting they had already met. The two coordinated room layouts in September 2018 (discussing bunking beds, fridges, and shelves), implying they shared a dorm room during freshman year.

[[people/sabrina]] was a home-based friend in Minnesota. The early exchanges are casual and affectionate. Sabrina and Maddie shared fitness interests from the start and spent time together during school breaks, going shopping, discussing training, and later (2019-2021) tubing, playing tennis, and studying together.

[[people/jon]], Maddie's older brother, appears from the first month of the record with messages that assume shared logistical dependence (the family car). He was already ahead of her in school and later in his startup timeline.

## Tone of the period

The 2018 messages are informal, reactive, and rooted in in-person proximity. The threads concern immediate logistics (where are you, come meet me, grab this from the store), shared social events, and the beginning of college social life. Maddie sent messages to Phoebe about being at breakfast, about carrying groceries, and about navigating campus. She confided in Sabrina about romantic situations. Jon coordinated practical household matters.

The period is distinct from later eras in its absence of startup discussion, professional networking, and long-distance relationship maintenance. These themes emerge gradually through 2019-2020 as the college years progress and geographic separation increases.

## Transition out of this era

By 2020, the world documented in the 2018 messages had changed substantially. Remote learning during COVID-19 reorganized social contact: the thread with Phoebe in May 2020 shows coursework and quiz collaboration conducted entirely over text. The thread with Sabrina in summer 2020 shows visits to Maddie's home for outdoor activities, which marks one of the last dense in-person activity clusters before Maddie's primary location appears to shift to the Bay Area. The thread with Jon expands in 2020 to include co-founder advice, incorporation logistics, and startup community recommendations, signaling Maddie's entry into the startup world.
