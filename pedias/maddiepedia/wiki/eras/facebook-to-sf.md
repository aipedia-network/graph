# Move to San Francisco (2018)

The period from late 2017 through mid-2018 marks Maddie Wang's transition from what appears to be a college or near-home context to full-time employment in the San Francisco Bay Area, most likely at Facebook. The earliest 1:1 messages with her father (chat_id 39) are dated January 2018 and establish a pattern of logistics coordination that suggests Maddie was already living away from the family's Minneapolis home, returning for breaks.

## Evidence of the Transition

Several early 2018 entries from the 1:1 chat with Maddie's father point to her being stationed in the Bay Area for a tech job:

- In January 2018, her father asked for a shipping address for a Panasonic charger, implying she had a separate address.
- In April 2018, he asked about BYU courses taken the prior year for income tax deduction purposes, suggesting she had completed some coursework recently.
- In May 2018, he texted: "Maddie, u need to ask face book travel counselor when you can bring one luggage for free for the flight they have booked for u." This is the clearest indicator of Facebook employment: a Facebook travel coordinator was booking flights for her.
- In May 2018, he coordinated family travel logistics around whether they should "drive to seattle from sanfrancisco or everyone flight to seattle," establishing that Maddie was based in the San Francisco area.
- In June 2018, he forwarded a Sun Country Airlines boarding pass from SFO to MSP (Minneapolis-St. Paul), confirming the SFO origin.

A July 2018 message from her father mentioned that "Shelly sent the graduation thank you card back" with photos, suggesting Maddie had recently graduated, placing her college graduation around spring 2018 or possibly 2017.

## Family Home Situation

In June 2018, while Maddie was at the airport gate preparing to fly home, her father mentioned a possible house sale: "If the house deal went through next week, we will need to move." Maddie's response was immediate: "I don't want to move. No one in the family wants to move except you." The exchange suggests the Minneapolis family home was under potential sale consideration during this period.

## Wang Family Chat Founding

The Wang Family group chat was created on November 23, 2018, with the message "New family chat." The timing of its creation after Maddie had been in San Francisco for several months suggests it was established as a new coordination layer once the family adjusted to having Maddie at a distance. The first substantive message Maddie sent in the chat on day one was: "If anyone's going to sephora, pick me up a mascara," a home-visit request indicating she was back in Minneapolis for the Thanksgiving weekend.

## Pattern Through 2019-2020

The early Wang Family entries (2018-2020) show Maddie visiting home regularly: Thanksgiving, Christmas, and summer visits are all reflected in car coordination threads and airport pickup messages. The 1:1 messages with her father during this period are mostly logistical: he coordinates tickets, tracks her arrivals, and handles administrative tasks from Minneapolis on her behalf. By 2021, the Wang Family chat volume is lower and the Gratitude Group opens, suggesting a more settled phase in which Maddie was operating independently in San Francisco with less logistical dependency on home.

## Significance

The 2018 era represents the beginning of the longest continuous geographic separation from her family in the documented record. Maddie went from a shared-car, home-based context (visible in 2018-2019 Minneapolis visit logistics) to a San Francisco resident who returned home for holidays and occasionally received dental bills and insurance guidance forwarded by her father. The Wang Family group chat and later the Gratitude Group were the digital infrastructure that maintained family connection across this distance.
