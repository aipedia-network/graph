# CoHo (Stanford Coffee House)

The CoHo, short for Coffee House, is a student-run coffee shop and casual gathering space on the Stanford University main campus. During [[Maddie Wang]]'s [[Stanford Era]], it functioned as a default meeting point and study location, particularly for Maddie and [[Amy Zhou]].

## Role During the Stanford Era

CoHo appears frequently in Maddie's fall 2022 messaging record as a shared destination for studying and casual hangouts. Coordination around it was informal and routine, with short exchanges such as "Coho?" answered by "Sure I can be there @5?" representing the typical pattern. The ease of these arrangements reflects CoHo's status as a reliable, low-overhead meeting point that both parties assumed without need for further explanation.

The space served both practical and social functions: a place to work alongside each other, to eat, and to catch up between classes and other commitments.

## Location and Context

CoHo is located near the center of the Stanford campus in Stanford, California. It is student-operated under the Stanford Student Enterprises umbrella and has been a campus institution for decades. Its proximity to [[Gates Computer Science Building]] and other frequently referenced locations in Maddie's record made it a convenient anchor point within the daily geography of campus life.
