# Gates Computer Science Building

The Gates Computer Science Building (officially the William Gates Computer Science Building) is Stanford University's primary computer science facility, housing faculty offices, labs, and study spaces. Maddie Wang and [[people/amy-zhou]] used it as a study location during the fall 2022 quarter. In September 2022, Amy messaged Maddie saying she would not make it to Gates that day, suggesting Gates was their default study destination. The two later coordinated around studying at Gates or at Coho depending on availability and plans.
