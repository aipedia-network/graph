# Zareen's

Zareen's is a restaurant on California Avenue in Palo Alto, California, near the Stanford University campus. It appears in Maddie Wang's messages from as early as 2019 through at least 2022 as a recurring social destination. The California Avenue location is walkable from the Stanford GSB area, approximately 33 minutes on foot; Maddie suggested meeting at Coupa Café at the GSB and walking over together.

## Role in Maddie's Social Life

Zareen's was one of Maddie's go-to dining destinations during her Stanford years. She organized and attended group dinners there frequently, inviting contacts including [[people/fransiska-hadiwidjana]], [[people/lainey]], and others. In October 2022, Maddie hosted a boba event at or affiliated with Zareen's, distributing boba and mango lassis to attendees. In November 2022, she referenced a plan to go to Zareen's with [[people/lainey]] after a campus event ("Sex then Socrates"), and invited [[people/amy-zhou]], who initially could not attend but later arrived.

A reference in early 2022 also mentions a second location in Mountain View. Maddie's messages show a strong preference for the California Avenue location, which she treated as a neighborhood anchor during the Stanford period.
