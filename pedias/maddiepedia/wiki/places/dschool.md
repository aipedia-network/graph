# d.school (Hasso Plattner Institute of Design)

The d.school is Stanford University's design institute, formally named the Hasso Plattner Institute of Design. Maddie Wang and [[people/amy-zhou]] both used it as a resource and social space during their time at Stanford. In September 2022, Amy messaged Maddie to alert her to free food and drinks at the d.school: "Maddie there is free food and drinks at the d.school. Let's go!" Maddie responded enthusiastically and went.

Amy described using the d.school regularly to access free supplies (pencils, pens, Post-it notes) and said she once acquired a laptop case left there by a visitor. The d.school hosted events, offered open design resources, and had a culture of accessible materials that students treated as communal. Maddie referenced the d.school alongside the Gates building as the pair's main campus work and social zones during the Stanford period.
