A dominant use of the chat is coordinating Maddie's arrivals and departures from Minneapolis. Messages like "Text us when you land," "Let us know which door," and "We are on our way" appear across 2018-2025, primarily from Maddie's father. During Maddie's college and early post-college years, the family shared one or two cars among multiple members. ## Tone and Communication Style

The early entries show the family learning to coordinate on a new platform, including a member's note on day one that "For now on" they could do group FaceTime instead of Skype. The tone is practical and logistical for most threads, occasionally warm. Humor appears around parental technology limitations (the prediction that "Baba will fall asleep" during Oppenheimer) and sibling teasing. Maddie's participation was more frequent in the 2018-2020 window, coinciding with her living at or near home. By 2023-2025, she checked in from a distance, texting landing confirmations and car schedules during holiday visits.

## Role in Maddie's Life

It functioned as the household logistics layer: car keys, airport gates, doctor appointments, Netflix passwords.
