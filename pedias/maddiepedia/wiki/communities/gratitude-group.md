# Gratitude Group

The Gratitude Group (styled in iMessage as "Gratitude Group❤️❤️❤️", chat_id 1043) is a family group chat that began in December 2021 as a Secret Santa coordination thread and by January 2022 had evolved into a broader channel for daily gratitude sharing, family celebrations, and extended-family communication. The chat remained active through at least April 2026, spanning 643 documented days.

## Origin

The chat was started on December 11, 2021, when a family member proposed a Secret Santa for Christmas. The opening message reads: "Hi family! For Christmas this year, is there interest in doing a secret Santa? If so, what do we want the budget to be?" The group settled on a $20 budget and used the Elfster app (elfster.com) to manage the exchange. Steph drew and distributed the names. The inaugural exchange took place on December 25, 2021.

## Evolution: From Secret Santa to Gratitude Practice

After the December 2021 exchange, a member proposed on January 15, 2022, that the group adopt a daily gratitude practice. The proposal came with a scientific framing ("Scientifically proven to make u feel happier everyday too") and immediate buy-in. Each member began posting three things they were grateful for, with members reacting with hearts and sharing their own lists in return. Maddie participated actively, sharing gratitude about friends, workouts, and family relationships. One member reported already journaling three gratitudes each morning and three highlights each night.

The daily practice was not sustained indefinitely, but the chat continued as a regular channel for sharing family news, celebrating birthdays, posting photos, and coordinating holiday plans.

## Holiday Coordination

The Gratitude Group became the primary channel for extended-family holiday coordination. Thanksgiving entries include food assignment lists, oven logistics, timing discussions, and real-time ETAs (2022: "ETA 6:45!"). Christmas entries show the group scheduling Secret Santa reveal calls via Google Meet, with time-zone negotiations across Minneapolis, New York, and other locations. Mother's Day (2023, 2025) brought photo shares and birthday messages. July 4th entries show members posting photos and remembering "Charlie" (an individual memorialized on Independence Day in 2022 with the message "I'll drink one for Charlie today").
