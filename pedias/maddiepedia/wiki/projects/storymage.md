# StoryMage

StoryMage (also styled storymage.ai) was Maddie Wang's LinkedIn ghostwriting and content strategy service, active from at least mid-2023 into early 2024. Maddie's work email was maddie@storymage.ai. The service used SocialPilot for post scheduling and approval workflows. The business produced LinkedIn posts for B2B founders and executives. Lainey was a collaborator on the service, contributing to content production, client feedback processes, and proposal writing.

## Service model

StoryMage offered LinkedIn content ghostwriting for B2B clients. The service launched its website at storymage.ai and onboarded clients through a page at storymage.ai/onboard. The documented client list includes founders identified as Andrew, Mac, Ethan Bull, Austin, and a client named Negin associated with "Disco." Additional named clients include a Pear VC partner named Aparna and a marketing agency founder described as shy but with strong case studies.

Maddie reported getting her first paying customer on September 7, 2023 at $800 per month. The service reached $4k MRR within two months of launch. By February 2024 Maddie reported $1.2k MRR from a new adjacent product. In August 2023 messages, Maddie referenced $263,500 earned through Founders Cafe (FC) and was developing process improvements. A client named Benjamin sent voice feedback in August 2023.

Pricing referenced in the messages: clients paid $300 per month (one new client mentioned in October 2023) and $2,000 per month in a proposed "Maddie Wang Consulting" structure. In February 2026, Maddie reported closing $150,000 ARR in two weeks.

## Content process

A detailed process discussion on October 5, 2023 between Maddie and Lainey documents the feedback workflow. The original approach had Maddie delivering draft posts on a call; clients found this difficult. Lainey proposed pre-sending drafts for clients to annotate before a feedback call. The triage system developed in that conversation used three categories: "Poop" (needs major rework), "Revise" (minor edits), and "Approved" (ready to schedule). The goal of the feedback call became: get through as many posts as possible, mark each, revise approvable ones on the spot, and schedule the approved set.

Lainey produced LinkedIn post drafts directly. In August 2023, she sent Maddie two formats for a founder named Philip (Philip of AI Endoscopy): a long-form narrative version and a shorter first-person storytelling format. Both were ghostwritten for a client identified as Ben.

## Client churn

At least one client, Ethan Bull, churned in early January 2024 after reporting that he got better results writing posts himself. Lainey observed that the posts had focused too heavily on EA (Effective Altruism) content rather than Ethan's personal voice, noting this was a goal-alignment failure. Maddie also referenced an ongoing concern about using Wayne Morris as an example client in sales calls after he stopped being a client.

## Intersection with Maddie and Lainey's relationship

StoryMage was a significant site of collaboration between Maddie and Lainey during what appears to be a period of cohabitation. Maddie asked Lainey for real-time help before sales calls, reviewed proposals with her, and relayed client wins by text. Lainey offered structured feedback and occasionally expressed frustration when pulled into support on processes she did not have full context for. The collaboration reflects the degree to which Maddie's professional and personal life were integrated with Lainey during 2023-2024.

Founders Cafe (FC) appears as a related organizational context. Maddie was affiliated with it and coordinated some StoryMage client relationships through it. Lainey used Maddie's Founders Cafe credentials (zecheesy email) to access the platform in August 2023 when she needed a VPN to use it from Chrome.
