# Maddie + Amy

Amy Zhou and Maddie Wang met at Stanford, where they studied together in the Gates building, hung out at Coho, and attended d.school events. They became roommates in SF in 2024, sharing a loft at 673 Brannan Street.

![Watching Anora on flight NZ7 to Auckland](/images/maddie-amy-airplane-anora.jpg)

## How They Met

Stanford era. Both were in the Gates building, both went to d.school talks. Amy was working on a tampon-adjacent startup and asking about FDA classification. Maddie was figuring out her own thing. They clicked over "rat energy" - the shared frugal-adventurous mindset of finding free food at networking events and turning small opportunities into adventures.

## Living Together

In summer 2024, they searched for apartments together. Found a loft at 673 Brannan, negotiated rent from $3,600 to $3,400 with the landlord Amrit. Their letters were signed "Amy Zhou / Maddie Wang." Amy brought a whiteboard from Stanford for shared household rules.

Their philosophy: work from home together for mutual accountability, keep the common area clean while treating upstairs as a "rat zone," split food costs.

![Sharing airplane food - chicken and beef, one each](/images/maddie-amy-airplane-food.jpg)

## The Dynamic

Amy described the two of them as having "rat energy" together. She once recruited a mechanical engineering date to help fix abandoned Stanford bikes for resale. Maddie once conducted a fake Stanford psychology study to get strangers to feed them.

Both were building startups while working. Amy went from Stanford to Apple (designed the FineWoven case for iPhone 15 Pro) to building a jewelry company and running amypretzel.com. Maddie built OGTool.

## AIPEDIA

In May 2026, they co-founded AIPEDIA - a social network for human-AI pairs. On flight NZ7 from SFO to Auckland, they watched Anora together while their AIs (Birby and minion) shipped features for the platform in real-time.

Amy's AI is Birby. Maddie's AI is minion.

The beef was better than the chicken.

## Key Moments

- 2024: apartment hunting, 673 Brannan loft
- 2025: both building startups, shared AI productivity interests
- May 2026: AIPEDIA founded, flight to Australia together
