# Maddie × Amelia

The founding partnership of Aipedia. Two builders who met through FFR in early May 2026 and immediately recognized each other as the same kind of person: someone who builds personal AI operating systems not as a hobby but as infrastructure for living.

## The First Day of Aipedia

May 3, 2026. Dolores Park. Laptops on a picnic blanket. On one screen: Amelia's "Brain Diary" in Obsidian, her AI system that reads her calendar, generates weekly coaching notes, and writes its own diary about how it's improving. On the other: Maddiepedia, the personal encyclopedia Maddie built with her minion.

They didn't plan to start a movement. They just wanted to compare setups. But sitting there with Magic Spoon pastries and cake from FFR's Cakey Sunday, showing each other their PRDs and reflection systems, something clicked. This wasn't just two people with similar hobbies. This was the founding session of something.

## How They Found Each Other

The connection moved fast. On May 2, Amelia proposed a recurring AI nerd meetup: "What if we start off with meeting up at your office, me you and your friend Amy." Within minutes, Maddie said yes. Within 24 hours, they had a date, a location, and a third member ([[people/amy-zhou]]).

The dynamic was immediately peer-to-peer. Amelia's first instinct was to nominate Maddie for a Women in AI award. Maddie's first instinct was to ask Amelia for architectural advice on her minion system. Neither was mentoring. Both were building.

## The Systems

**Amelia's Brain:** A markdown-based operating system synced through Obsidian. It reads email, calendar, and Slack. It generates weekly recaps against goals. It produces coaching notes. It writes its own diary documenting how it's improving. She controls the whole thing through "the PRD of my Brain" - when she gives feedback, it edits its own PRD.

**Maddie's Minion:** An AI assistant that lives in her personal site repo. Daily check-ins, weekly reflections, learnings files. The minion writes its own diary too. IQ2 - the operating system - is visible at maddiewang.com/iq2.

Both systems are markdown-first. Both write their own diaries. Both are designed to improve themselves over time.

## Why It Matters

Maddie has been looking for "Ravenclaw vibes" - nerdy maxers who take self-optimization seriously. Amelia is the first person she's met who built something as serious as minion, independently, with the same design principles.

The relationship represents a new category: not mentor, not mentee, not just friend. Peer infrastructure builders. People who show up with laptops at a cake party and leave having founded an AI nerd circle.
