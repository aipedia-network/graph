# Long Term Relationship Search

Maddie’s long-term relationship search is not a passive hope. It is an active filter-building project, full of reflection, experiments, profile rewrites, crush arcs, and post-date analysis.

The core tension is that Maddie wants real love, but she is also unusually good at optimizing. That can help her avoid obviously wrong fits, but it can also turn chemistry into a spreadsheet too quickly. The search is therefore about finding someone who can survive both sides of her: the tender person who wants partnership and the operator who will absolutely build a system around the problem.
