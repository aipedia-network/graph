# Maddie and Lainey

Maddie Wang and Lainey maintained a 1,251-day texting record spanning December 2019 through May 2026, making Lainey Maddie's most-messaged contact across the entire dataset. The relationship moved through a romantic partnership phase, a collaborative work phase, a crisis and rupture, and then a sustained post-romantic friendship.

## Pet names and in-group language

The most consistent feature of the messages is an elaborate shared vocabulary built around bugs and potatoes. Lainey called Maddie "buggy," "bug," "bugbug," "bubbers," "bup bup," and "chonky potato." Maddie called Lainey "hunny bug," "bugbug," and "blub" or "blubby." Both used third-person constructions to name their own states ("bugbug was really looking forward to hanging out"; "buggy loves you"), a pattern that appears as early as 2021 and continues through 2026. The first documented exchange, December 28, 2019, consists of nothing but two emoji: a potato (Lainey) and a chestnut (Maddie). The register is consistent regardless of whether the topic is logistics, emotion, conflict, or business.

## Communication style

Messages are frequent and range from single-emoji exchanges to long collaborative threads. Lainey's texts tend to be slightly longer and more structured when giving feedback or coordinating. Maddie's texts tend to be shorter and more fragmented, often sending several two-to-three word messages in sequence. Both use "lol," "lmao," "omg," and typo-laden casual prose throughout. Lainey uses "o.O" and ":3" as recurring emoticons.

Business and logistics discussions are interleaved with affection. On January 2, 2024, Maddie addressed Lainey formally by name ("Also lainey! Would u be willing to take over 2 sales calls") before returning to the "blub" register within the same conversation. The boundaries between the professional and the personal are consistently blurred across the 2022-2024 period, when they were co-working on Maddie's StoryMage startup.
