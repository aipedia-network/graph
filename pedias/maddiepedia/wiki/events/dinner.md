# Dinner

Dinner is not one event in Maddie's archive so much as a recurring social format: small enough to feel intimate, structured enough to create momentum, and flexible enough to become dating, friendship, founder networking, or emotional repair depending on who is at the table.

It belongs in Maddiepedia because many of Maddie's important arcs do not happen in formal meetings. They happen in side conversations, meals after events, boba runs, potlucks, and dinners that turn weak ties into actual relationships.
