# Car Towed — January 5, 2024

In the early morning hours of January 5, 2024, Maddie Wang discovered that her car had been towed while she was out singing in San Francisco. She initially believed the car might have been stolen and spent approximately an hour searching the area before concluding it had been towed.

## Circumstances

Maddie texted [[people/lainey]] at 5:23 AM, writing "My car got stolen / Or towed / I can't find it." She described "walking around for an hour after singing" with 6% phone battery remaining, and said she was heading to a police station. The message was sent from San Francisco.

Her partner (Lainey) looked up the situation remotely and confirmed within minutes that the car had been towed. The tow company's automated notification system indicated the reason as "ran out of toe time," meaning the car had exceeded its allowed parking duration. The vehicle was held at a facility at 450 7th Street in San Francisco, across from the San Francisco Sheriff's Department, reachable at 415-865-8200. The facility operates 24 hours a day.

## Response

Lainey advised Maddie to prioritize charging her phone and getting safely home, noting they could retrieve the car together the following day. Maddie confirmed the tow situation from the tow company around 6 AM and was en route to investigate. By midday, the car appears to have been retrieved, as Maddie sent a photo with the caption "Reunited" to the Wang Family group chat that evening.

## Context

January 5, 2024 was an active day in Maddie's social calendar. The Maddie's Birthday Bonanza group chat was running simultaneously, with friends planning a birthday weekend trip including an Airbnb, wine tasting, and other activities. Maddie also had a scheduled meeting that day with Siraj Nazir ([[projects/ivylevel]]) to discuss pricing and LinkedIn content. The car towing was a disruption in an otherwise busy day.

Lainey's reaction that morning focused on Maddie's safety before the logistical problem, writing: "Most important thing is making sure my buggy is home and safe."
