# Amelia Lin

Amelia Lin is an AI builder and one of Maddie Wang's newest close peers in the SF Era. Phone: (469) 222-0841 (Dallas area code). Email: Amelia@vcbackedmoms.com. LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/amelialin/. The two connected in early May 2026 through the FFR community after meeting at an AI marketing session, and within the first 24 hours of texting had committed to starting an "AI nerd circle" with [[people/amy-zhou]].

## Personal AI Operating System

Amelia's defining trait in the conversation is her self-built personal AI operating system, run entirely on markdown files synced through Obsidian. The system reads her email, calendar, and Slack; generates weekly recap reports against her goals; produces coaching notes for the week ahead; and writes its own diary documenting how it is improving. She controls the whole thing through a single PRD-style markdown file she described as "the PRD of my Brain." When she gives the system feedback, it edits its own PRD. It also keeps an ongoing learnings file per topic that it can reference in perpetuity. She quoted one of its self-generated observations to Maddie verbatim: "I'm noticing a pattern which is Amelia exercises less in busy weeks 😂."

When Maddie asked whether to store her own equivalent system in Supabase or in markdown files on GitHub, Amelia argued for GitHub on the grounds of universality: every AI tool will optimize for it, code and notes can live in the same repo, and cross-repo access will be solved by AI. "My gut vote is GH." Maddie loved the message and reversed her own direction toward markdown.
