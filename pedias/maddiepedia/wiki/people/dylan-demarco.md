# Dylan DeMarco

Dylan DeMarco is Maddie Wang's executive function coach and accountability partner. Phone: +1 (857) 270-8735. Venmo: @ddemarco. They have weekly calls on Sundays.

## The Coaching Relationship

Dylan started coaching Maddie in April 2025 with a homework-heavy onboarding: a summary doc, expected calendar blocks, a time audit, and a distraction pad. When Maddie asked about inbox zero, Dylan reframed it immediately: "inbox zero ≠ all things responded to." He taught her to sort, schedule, and prioritize rather than respond to everything. "There are rarely more than two things that are truly a 1 in a given day."

Within days, Maddie reported the distraction pad worked. She noticed less overwhelm. She stopped doing every thought immediately. Dylan compared her to "an olympic-level sprinter moving from running on sand to a track with some good shoes." The coaching was working.

## The Buddhist Middle Path

When Maddie asked what to do when distraction-pad thoughts arise during meditation, Dylan shared his background: he nearly went to a monastery in Nepal before Harvard. His answer was flexible, not rigid: "There's almost always a middle path." Keep a notebook nearby if needed. Practice with thoughts rather than fighting them.

## The System That Worked

Dylan helped Maddie set up Google Sheets task views: active tasks, trophy case, top-ranked priorities. Completed tasks get ranked 999 to push them to the bottom. The trophy case celebrates what's done.

The results were immediate. "YAY it works!" Then: "So grateful for you. I got SO MUCH DONE! I felt so happy 🥹"

A week later, Maddie reported she was "300x more productive," closed two deals, and moved from 20k to 26k MRR. Dylan pushed her toward brutal selectivity: "Time is by far the most valuable resource you have." Even saving one second on five daily emails can justify hours of automation over time.
