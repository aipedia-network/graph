# Amy Zhou

Amy Zhou is a Stanford-era friend of [[people/maddie-wang]] and one of Maddie's closest long-term contacts in the record. The two met during their overlapping time at Stanford University, where they studied together in the Gates building, attended events at the d.school, and socialized at Coho (the Stanford coffee house). Their conversation thread (chat_id 1353, phone +13093398832) spans 627 days across 2022 through May 2026.

## Background

Amy was a student at Stanford during the 2022-2023 period. She described herself as working on a startup involving a tampon-adjacent product at that time, seeking advice from FDA-related advisors about medical device classification. She used the d.school frequently as a resource and workspace, and also attended talks on product design and the sexual wellness industry. She expressed strong interest in product design and entrepreneurship from early in the documented record.

By September 2023, Amy had joined Apple, where she worked on a product released to market: a FineWoven case for the iPhone 15 Pro. She described her role as design-related. In 2024 she was simultaneously working at Apple while exploring a startup, and asked Maddie whether working on a startup while employed at Apple carried risk. By 2025, she was working on multiple projects including a jewelry company and a blog about mechanical engineering and AI, operating a site at amypretzel.com.

## Living with Maddie

In summer 2024, Amy and Maddie co-searched for an apartment in San Francisco. They toured a loft at 673 Brannan Street, unit 302, and negotiated with the landlord (Amrit) to reduce rent from $3,600 to $3,400 per month. Their letters to the landlord were signed "Amy Zhou / Maddie Wang." They described themselves as "women in tech" and leveraged connections to previous tenants who were mutual friends (Q and Fiona). Amy brought a whiteboard from Stanford campus to use for shared household rules.

Amy and Maddie discussed their shared living philosophy in detail: working from home together for mutual accountability, maintaining a clean common area while treating upstairs as a "rat zone," and splitting food costs. Amy mentioned planning to commute via Caltrain, bike, and skateboard. She had two bikes she had acquired from Stanford, one of which she kept.

## Personality and Interests

Amy identified herself as resourceful and entrepreneurially minded. She described a date where she enlisted a mechanical engineering date to help acquire and fix abandoned Stanford bikes before selling them on Facebook Marketplace for $750. She referred to this approach, and the broader practice of finding free food at networking events, as "rat energy," a term she and Maddie used as an affectionate label for their shared frugal-adventurous mindset.

She was interested in AI, building her own personal AI operating system and sharing prompting techniques with Maddie. In April 2026, Amy introduced Maddie to the Claude Code /frontend-skill and Supabase API for a shared wiki project. She maintained a site at amypretzel.com. By 2026, both Maddie and Amy were active in AI productivity circles in San Francisco.
