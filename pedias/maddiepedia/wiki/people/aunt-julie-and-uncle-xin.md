# Aunt Julie and Uncle Xin

Aunt Julie and Uncle Xin are members of the [[communities/gratitude-group]] and part of the extended family network that communicates with Maddie Wang. They are addressed as "aunt" and "uncle" by family members and by Maddie directly. Their presence in the Gratitude Group is consistent from the chat's early weeks in January 2022 through at least 2023.

## Aunt Julie

Julie is identified in several ways across the messages. Her birthday falls on April 25, and her approach to age 60 is a running reference in 2022-2023. On April 25, 2022, the Gratitude Group collectively celebrated her birthday. One member sent a raspberry champagne cake from Kowalski's, recalling that Julie had mentioned Kowalski's cakes in the past. Julie responded to the birthday flood with characteristic humor: "I am very touched this year from everyone, including my two nannies since they gave me a surprised party. Thank you all so much! Just so you all know this is not my 60th birthday yet! This year is just a test run for next year's mega 60th birthday celebration!!"

Maddie sent a separate message: "AWWWWW so happy u got a surprise party. u deserve it you are such an amazing, caring person who puts her all into everything she does. Love u."

Julie was working toward a CFP (Certified Financial Planner) certification during the documented period. On December 26, 2022, she posted to the group that "My last project was finally submitted today!! Yay! Thanks for everyone helping me cooking - first time do not need to cook during holiday or tolerating my temper. Special thanks to aunt Jenny and uncle Xin!!" Her message indicates she was managing a demanding professional credential exam while also hosting or participating in the family Christmas gathering.

In the Mother's Day 2023 entry, Julie received a Hermes scarf from a family gift-giver and responded: "Because the giver has a good taste! Thank you to my giver!!" on Mother's Day, indicating she has children.

## Uncle Xin

Uncle Xin is mentioned several times in the Gratitude Group entries. In March 2022, a family member shared gratitude for "being able to go ice fishing last weekend and enjoy eating fish with friends," with Uncle Xin referenced as one of the fish-eating participants. In the same thread, Aunt Jenny told Uncle Xin he should wear a girdle so he could save half the fish for her next time, in a joking exchange. In November 2022, Uncle Xin's sushi is anticipated with enthusiasm: "I want to go to the escape room and eat uncle xin's sushi!!!!" He is referenced alongside Aunt Jenny as having taken over cooking duties during the Christmas 2022 holiday.

Uncle Xin appears to be one of the more food-focused figures in the extended family, with multiple references to his cooking and his participation in eating and ice fishing activities.

## Aunt Jenny

Aunt Jenny is referenced indirectly as a separate person from Aunt Julie, appearing in the ice fishing thread (she wished she had been invited), in the cooking assistance at Christmas 2022, and in a February 2022 reference to playing Cards Against Humanity ("the time we played Cards Against Humanity with Aunt Jenny. Best night ever actually"). The name Jenny is distinguished from Julie throughout the messages, indicating these are two different relatives.

## Extended Family Network

Taken together, Aunt Julie, Uncle Xin, and Aunt Jenny form part of the extended family tier visible in the Gratitude Group. They appear to be based in or near Minneapolis. The Kowalski's cake reference, the ice fishing outing, and the shared holiday gatherings all point to a tight-knit local family cluster. Their relationship to Maddie's immediate family (whether Julie and Jenny are sisters of one of Maddie's parents, or connected through another branch) is not specified in the available messages.
