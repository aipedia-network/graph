# Jueihua Chang

Jueihua Chang is Maddie Wang's mother. She appears across direct and family threads as one of the strongest sources of family logistics, care, worry, planning, and practical support.

## Mother and family operator

Jueihua's messages cover travel, insurance, doctors, dental care, phone plans, tax documents, flights, hotel rooms, restaurant reservations, family meetings, food, birthday trips, Thanksgiving logistics, and reminders. She often addresses Maddie, Jon, and Allison together. Her care frequently appears as operational urgency.

In early Stanford-era messages she wrote, “Maddie, love you back thousand times!!! Text us when u are in pheonix and stanford.” Other representative lines include “Call me now,” “dad will come to pick u up now,” and “I am trying to make salad but wonder u need it or not.”

## Emotional core

Jueihua is not just logistics. In April 2022 she wrote about reading her children's letter and crying because every word felt from their hearts. She wondered whether she had been “a good mother” and wrote that she was proud her children followed their passions and wanted to make impact.

This emotional layer gives context to Maddie's own planning instincts. Jueihua models care as preparation, risk management, and constant attention.

## Role in Maddie's life

Jueihua is Maddie's mother, anxious protector, food-and-travel planner, and family root. Her thread helps explain Maddie's relationship to logistics, risk, achievement, and care.
