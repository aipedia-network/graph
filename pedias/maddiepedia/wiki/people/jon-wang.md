# Jon Wang

Jon Wang is Maddie Wang's older brother. He is identified by email address jon.wang222@gmail.com in family group chats and phone number +16512853335 (Minnesota area code). He texted Maddie across 857 conversation-days from January 2018 through at least April 2026, the longest individual thread in the documented record. He is based in New York City for most of this period and is the older of Maddie's two siblings, with sister [[people/allison-wang]] being the youngest.

## Sibling relationship

Jon addressed Maddie as "little sister" in multiple exchanges, including her January 2019 birthday: "Happy birthday little sister. Love you!" The earliest messages, from January 2018, center on a shared family car in Minnesota. Jon texted urgently when Maddie had the car keys at her dorm: "Do you have the car keys ????". These exchanges suggest both were living at or near home during parts of 2018 before settling separately — Maddie in the Bay Area, Jon in New York.

In September 2019, during what appeared to be a difficult period for Maddie, he wrote: "take some time to process if you need it. here for you little sister."

His partner Annie first appears in family messages around late 2022-2023; by November 2024 she is included in Thanksgiving logistics alongside Jon.

## Startup involvement

Jon founded Shimmer, a mental health technology company, by 2020. He described it as "building tech to deliver more personalized mental health care in easily accessible ways" and sent Maddie the URL shimmer.care in September 2020. The siblings exchanged startup operational notes around the same time: co-founder dynamics, incorporation lawyers, equity splits. Jon sent his July 2023 message "sent email about angel round info," indicating Shimmer reached an angel fundraising stage.

Jon recommended startup community resources to Maddie (OnDeck, YC Find a Founder) in January 2021 when she was looking for a co-founder. In April 2023 both attended an Asian Founder event together. In early 2025 he bought an AI photoshoot for a customer and reported the results to Maddie.
