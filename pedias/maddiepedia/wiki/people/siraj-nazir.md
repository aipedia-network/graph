# Siraj Nazir

Siraj Nazir (+14155319221) is a Bay Area entrepreneur and the founder of [[projects/ivylevel]], a college admissions mentorship startup. He and Maddie Wang collaborated from May 2023 through February 2024.

## Background

Nazir described his career path as moving from fintech to foodtech to edtech. He has a daughter named Huda and at least one son. His daughter's experience in STEM, including social difficulties in a school CS environment and a subsequent CS award win, formed the emotional core of his marketing narrative for IvyLevel. He described IvyLevel as "a FEMINIST Dad's startup."

He is South Asian and oriented his business model around reaching South Asian, East Asian, and international parent communities who, in his characterization, were willing to pay high premiums for elite college preparation. He frequently analyzed "WTP" (willingness to pay) by customer persona, distinguishing between domestic parents and wealthier international families.

## Relationship with Maddie

Nazir first messaged Maddie in May 2023, shortly after an in-person meeting. He described looking forward to meeting "rich Asian parents" together, referring to prospective customers. In his first message he signed on to Maddie's framing of the mentorship pitch and suggested signing up his own daughter.

Over the following months he worked intensively with Maddie, often messaging in late-night bursts. He described running on very little sleep and having trouble shutting off his brain when excited about product developments. He frequently over-communicated by his own acknowledgment, sending detailed strategic memos via text. Maddie offered product feedback, pricing advice, and occasional pushback; Nazir described the dynamic as "best mentor-mentee" and valued her willingness to challenge his ideas.

By January 2024 he referred to Maddie as his "cofounder" in a public LinkedIn post, though no formal co-founder agreement is documented. He mentioned her in a post about his daughter's CS win: "I have shared my daughter's struggles with few of my female ex colleagues, my cofounder, Stanford coaches."

## End of collaboration

The collaboration ended February 17, 2024. Nazir sent a detailed message referencing a December 2023 text as the inflection point when Maddie had indicated she lacked bandwidth for the CRO role. He contended that she had never fully committed, cited specific payments including an initial $1,000 trust payment, and said "You were actually never in this." Maddie responded that she did not have energy for the conversation and asked for space. Nazir offered reconciliation if Maddie chose to re-engage in the future.

## LinkedIn presence

Nazir was an active LinkedIn content creator and used the platform as his primary acquisition channel. His LinkedIn profile URL contains the handle "sirajnazir." He posted about his daughter, immigrant parent pain points, college prep market dynamics, and lessons from building IvyLevel. By January 2024 he reported strong organic traction from posts targeting middle school parent audiences.
