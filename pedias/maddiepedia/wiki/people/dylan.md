# Dylan

Dylan is Maddie's accountability contact, associated with the recurring weekly review system she uses to keep founder life from dissolving into solo overthinking.

The archive contains a few unrelated Dylans, so the useful Maddiepedia thread is narrower: CrossFit Dylan, the person Maddie identified as part of her return to structured accountability. In September 2025, Maddie wrote, "Hey dylan!!! I’m ready to come back. I increased my MRR so I can afford to pay more." The message is revealing because the purchase is not just coaching. It is Maddie buying a container for execution at the moment OGTool has enough revenue to justify more support.

Her ask was specific. She wanted help "sticking with the systems u build, and staying accountable," and she said she liked Dylan's approach. By January 2026, Dylan had become shorthand in her own planning notes: "crossfit dylan" and "Find the crossfit dylan for the weekly review."

Dylan's role in the wiki is therefore functional and relational at once. He represents one of Maddie's external scaffolds: a person who turns ambition into review cadence, promises, and follow-through.
