# Ngoc

Ngoc is a Stanford-era friend and roommate of Maddie Wang, and one of the three members of the [[communities/stanford-queer-girls]] group chat (chat_id 1183, phone +16504401196). Ngoc graduated from Stanford in June 2022 and was a TA in at least one course during her final quarter, where she covered office hours when asked. She was also active in music, attending regular music rehearsals and practices in the Mission Bay area of San Francisco during summer 2022.

## Background

Ngoc is Vietnamese and has family in Vietnam. In May 2022 she asked Maddie and Lainey if her youngest sister could stay temporarily at their shared apartment because of political instability at home, describing the situation as "really volatile." The request was granted. Ngoc's work status in summer 2022 was contingent on receiving OPT (Optional Practical Training) authorization, which was delayed; she described the situation as "crying in US bureaucracy." She held a position starting that summer, likely in tech or a related field, and was attending to new-employee paperwork.

## Shared Housing at 1540 Fell Street

Ngoc was one of three co-tenants at 1540 Fell Street in San Francisco, along with Maddie and Lainey. She participated actively in the apartment search, expressing a preference for a unit with in-unit laundry and indicating she was willing to pay $100 more per month for it. She helped coordinate finances, offering to wire her share of the deposit to Maddie after the group confirmed the lease. She identified logistical details in the apartment search: laundromats near the building, the building's absence of a fourth floor (culturally significant, she noted, given associations of the number four in Chinese culture), and cost estimates for utilities.

Ngoc handled household communication clearly and preemptively, which Maddie commented on in May 2022: "I don't know anyone w better communication skills than u Ngoc ur amazing." When Ngoc was exposed to COVID in June 2022, she notified the group immediately with full details of her exposure, planned isolation location, and offers of rapid tests to her roommates.

Ngoc regularly attended music rehearsals in San Francisco, which affected her weekend scheduling, and traveled to Mission Bay for practice. She was part of a musical group or ensemble that held performances at Stanford and in the city.
