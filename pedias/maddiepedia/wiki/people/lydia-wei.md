# Lydia Wei

Lydia Wei is a Stanford friend, writer, and mentee-like confidante in Maddie Wang's archive. Lydia described the origin of their relationship as a “senior-frosh big sib pairing,” writing that meeting Maddie that way was “such a miracle.”

## Stanford big-sib relationship

The thread runs from 2022 through 2026, with an earlier phone-number thread from 2020-2022 that appears to belong to Lydia as well. Lydia treats Maddie as an older friend, mentor, audience, and emotional interpreter. Maddie receives Lydia's updates with a mixture of analysis, encouragement, and delighted participation.

## Writing, queerness, and emotional life

Lydia's messages are literary, funny, and emotionally explicit. Early material includes Hong Kong, quarantine, travel, Mandarin, sex, friendships, missing Stanford, and wanting to “poop around the world writing, traveling, learning languages.” The older thread places Lydia in feminist studies, race and ethnic studies, creative writing, and Homegirl Project. She calls Maddie a “CS entrepreneur legend” and “my guru.”

Lydia also gives Maddie serious writing feedback. In April 2026 she helped with Maddie's relationship ROI story, telling her that if the graph led to an actual change, “then that’s more of a story.”

## Role in Maddie's life

Lydia is younger-sister energy, writer friend, queer Stanford confidante, and a mirror for Maddie's mentor identity. The relationship shows Maddie as someone who collects brilliant younger weirdos and helps them narrate themselves.
