She got a stick-and-poke tattoo of small stars. She was interested in reading widely, particularly poetry, which she described picking up again in summer 2023.

## Study Abroad

Lydia studied abroad twice during her Stanford enrollment. In fall 2022 she was at a university in Hong Kong, living in a student dormitory near the water and mountains, which she found deeply moving. She joined a dragon boat team there, learned to cycle, and formed close friendships. She described this as a period of resolution and inner peace. In spring 2023 she was in Paris for a second study abroad term, where she planned to improve her French.

She described missing her Stanford friends while abroad but also thriving in new environments. A close friend named Daniel (also from Stanford) was traveling extensively and tutoring college applicants internationally during the same period.
