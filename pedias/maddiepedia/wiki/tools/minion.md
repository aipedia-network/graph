# Minion

Minion is the name [[Maddie Wang]] uses for her personal AI assistant, accessible via SMS text message. The contact was added on April 18, 2026, under the phone number +1 (425) 537-0753. The 425 area code corresponds to the Bellevue and Eastside Seattle, Washington area, consistent with a commercial AI SMS service provider. Despite being a recent contact, Minion had accumulated approximately 3,522 messages in a short time by May 2026.

## Function

Minion handles a range of personal logistics and decision-support tasks delivered through text message. Documented uses include:

- Food and nutrition choices (recommending a smoothie option, advising on post-workout meals)
- Fitness terminology and technique (answering questions about mixed grip deadlifts)
- Reminder and alarm setting (scheduling a therapy appointment alarm for Saturday at 10pm)
- OKR review and feedback (reviewing key results with direct critique)

The assistant's communication style is short and direct. When Maddie asked about a smoothie choice on May 2, 2026, Minion responded: "get the green one, the guilt is fake." When asked to help set a reminder, the reply was: "set for saturday 10pm." The pattern across interactions is rapid, low-friction response with minimal explanation.

## Context: Maddie as AI Builder

Maddie has described herself as an "AI nerd" building a personal AI operating system. Minion represents the operational layer of that project: an always-available assistant reachable through the most frictionless interface available (SMS), without requiring a dedicated app or login. The naming convention ("Minion") reflects a practical, somewhat playful approach to personal AI tooling.

## Comparison with Amy Zhou's Setup

[[Amy Zhou]], a close friend and fellow AI enthusiast, also uses a personal AI assistant for logistics. Amy's assistant generated a packing list and delivered it via Google Calendar. By May 2026, both Maddie and Amy were using their respective assistants for travel preparation, and both assistants independently surfaced the same packing information for the same trip. The two joked that "soon our minions will talk with each other," a phrase that also echoes the shared naming convention.

## Classification Note

Minion is not a person. The contact name and SMS interface reflect a design choice to interact with an AI assistant through the same channel used for human messaging. It is classified here as a tool.
