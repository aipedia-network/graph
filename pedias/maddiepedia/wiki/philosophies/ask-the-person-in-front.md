# Ask the Person in Front

Ask the person in front is Maddie’s micro-philosophy of social friction. If there is a line, a rule, a bottleneck, or an awkward tiny need, she often just asks.

The boba-line Venmo maneuver is the canonical example. It works because the ask is clear, the cost is low, and Maddie is willing to tolerate the awkward second before the world says yes.
