# Build the Thing Instead of Confessing

Build the thing instead of confessing is one of Maddie’s funniest and most diagnostic philosophies. When normal vulnerability feels too exposed, she sometimes routes desire through projects: websites, clubs, systems, sports, graphs.

The pattern can be charming and genuinely generative. It can also be a dodge. The wiki keeps it as a named philosophy because it explains why Maddie’s romantic life and builder instincts are so entangled.
