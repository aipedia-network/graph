# Connection Maxing

Connection maxing is Maddie’s habit of treating relationships as something that can be cultivated deliberately without pretending they are purely transactional. The phrase belongs to her founder life, dating life, and friend networks at the same time.

At its best, connection maxing means noticing promising people, following up, creating reasons to meet, and turning one conversation into a larger graph. At its worst, it risks turning aliveness into logistics. That tension is why it belongs in Maddiepedia: it explains both Maddie’s unusual social effectiveness and the moments where the system starts to eat the feeling.
