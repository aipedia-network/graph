# Queer Founder Social Graph

The queer founder social graph is the overlapping world where Maddie’s dating life, friend life, and company-building life keep colliding. Founder events become crush territory. Crushes become LinkedIn contacts. Social gatherings become distribution channels, emotional laboratories, and occasionally product inspiration.

This pattern is not incidental. Maddie is drawn to ambitious weirdos, especially people who understand growth, agency, queerness, and building. The graph is powerful because it creates density. It is dangerous because every node can start to feel strategically important.
