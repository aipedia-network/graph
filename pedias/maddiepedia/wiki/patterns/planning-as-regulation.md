# Planning as Regulation

Planning is one of Maddie’s emotional regulation tools. Calendars, weekly reviews, anxieties lists, and accountability calls are not only productivity artifacts. They are ways to make a fast-moving inner life legible enough to act on.

The pattern has a double edge. Planning helps Maddie stop spiraling alone and convert vague dread into next actions. But over-planning can also become a substitute for contact with the thing itself: the date, the risk, the conversation, the lift, the launch.
