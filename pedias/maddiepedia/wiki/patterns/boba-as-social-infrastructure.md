# Boba as Social Infrastructure

Boba is a recurring unit of social coordination in Maddie’s life. It is small enough to be casual, appealing enough to create momentum, and structured enough to turn “we should hang out” into an actual plan.

The best example is Maddie’s habit of asking people at the front of the line if she can add her order to theirs and Venmo them. It is absurdly efficient and socially revealing. Maddie treats friction as negotiable, and boba becomes proof that many barriers are softer than they look.
